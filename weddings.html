Wedding & Bridal Shower Cakes in Tampa

Vintage-style wedding cakes, plus cakes for the engagement party, the shower and the first anniversary.

How booking works

  • Booking window. I take orders up to 1½ months before your pickup date. Dates further out can't be confirmed yet.
  • Deposit. A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves your date. The rest is due 24 hours before pickup.
  • Pickup and delivery. Cakes are made for pickup. Delivery is very limited and has a mileage fee, so confirm it before you book.
  • Heat. Buttercream softens in the Florida heat. Plan an air-conditioned ride and a cool, shaded spot at the venue.

Wedding cakes are ivory or white, round or heart-shaped, with swag and drape piping, pearls and bows to match your colors. Cakes start at $65 for a 6-inch, 2-layer cake. See all prices, flavors and add-ons.

Wedding cake questions

Do you make tiered wedding cakes?

Yes, two-tier cakes like the ivory one on this page, round or heart-shaped. Tell me your guest count and I'll suggest a size.

Can I book my wedding cake more than 1½ months ahead?

Not yet. I only confirm orders within 1½ months of the pickup date. You're welcome to ask questions earlier, then send your quote request once you're inside that window.

Do you deliver to venues?

Delivery is very limited, isn't guaranteed and has a mileage fee. Confirm it with me before you book. Otherwise, cakes are made for pickup.
