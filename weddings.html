Booking Your Wedding Cake

  • Reach out early. The sooner you send your date, the better your chances of getting it.
  • Deposit. A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your date. The balance is due 24 hours before pickup.
  • Pickup & delivery. Cakes are made for pickup. Delivery is very limited and has a mileage fee, so confirm it before booking.
  • Florida heat. Buttercream is heat-sensitive. Plan for a cool, shaded spot at your venue and an air-conditioned ride.

Wedding Cake FAQ

Do you make tiered wedding cakes?

Yes. I make two-tier vintage wedding cakes like the ones in my gallery, in heart or round. Tell me your guest count and I'll recommend the right size and setup.

How do I reserve my wedding date?

Send a quote request with your date. Once you're ready to book, a 50% non-refundable deposit secures your date. The balance is due 24 hours before pickup. A quote alone doesn't hold your date.

Do you deliver to wedding venues?

Delivery is very limited and not guaranteed. When it's available, there's an extra fee based on mileage. Please confirm delivery with me before you book. Otherwise, cakes are made for pickup.

Can you make a cake for our engagement party or bridal shower?

Absolutely. Bride-to-be, just-engaged and just-married heart cakes are some of my favorites to make.
