The Cake Everyone's Asking For

Vintage heart cakes are my specialty. Each one is a heart-shaped cake frosted in smooth buttercream and piped by hand with layer after layer of shells, swags, ruffles and scallops, the classic Lambeth look. Then come the finishing touches: satin bows, fresh or glitter cherries, edible pearls and your message written across the top.

They're perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, engagements, bridal showers, Galentine's and Valentine's Day, or any day that deserves a little extra. Pick any color palette, from soft pink and ivory to bold red, black or chocolate.

Heart Cake FAQ

What is a vintage heart cake?

A vintage heart cake is a heart-shaped buttercream cake decorated in a retro, over-the-top piping style: layered shell borders, swags, ruffles and scrolls, usually finished with bows, cherries or pearls. It's the look that's taken over Instagram and TikTok, and it's what I love to make most.

Is a vintage cake the same as a Lambeth cake?

Pretty much. Lambeth is the name of the classic overpiping technique the vintage style comes from. When people ask for a Lambeth cake, a vintage cake or a retro heart cake, they usually mean the same thing: lots of layered buttercream piping.

What sizes do your heart cakes come in?

Heart cakes come in 6, 8 and 10 inch sizes with 2, 3 or 4 layers, starting at $65. See the full price list. Not sure what size you need? Put your guest count on the quote form and I'll recommend one.

Can I get a round vintage cake instead?

Yes. Every design can be made heart-shaped or round, at the same starting prices.

Can you write a custom message on it?

Yes. Standard writing is included, and 3D lettering is available as an add-on. Tell me the exact wording on the quote form.

Can I send an inspiration photo?

Yes. Tap any cake in the gallery and choose "Get a quote for one like this" to attach it to your request. For photos from elsewhere, message me on Instagram.
