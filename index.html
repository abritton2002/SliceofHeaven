How to Order Your Custom Cake

  1. Pick a date. I take orders up to 1½ months before your pickup date and at least 3 days out. Dates further than 1½ months away can't be confirmed yet.
  2. Request a quote. Send your date, guest count, colors and message through the quote form. "Not sure yet" is fine for anything you haven't decided.
  3. Book it. I reply within 24 to 48 hours with your quote. Once you receive your quote, a 50% non-refundable deposit is due to secure your date, and the rest is due 24 hours before pickup.
  4. Pick up your cake. Cakes are made for pickup at your scheduled time. Delivery is very limited, so ask before you book.
