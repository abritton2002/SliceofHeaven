Planning a reveal or shower cake

Pick your colors (pink and blue, pastels or all ivory) and the words on top, like "Boy or Girl?", "baby in bloom" or the baby's name. Heart and round cakes both work, and a two-tier cake is an option for a bigger shower.

Choose Gender Reveal or Other (for showers) as the occasion on the quote form. Cakes start at $65 for a 6-inch, 2-layer cake. See all prices, flavors and add-ons.

Gender reveal questions

Can the reveal color be inside the cake?

Ask for it in your quote request and I'll tell you what's possible for your design.

Can someone else order so the answer stays secret?

Yes. Whoever knows the answer can fill out the quote form and put the reveal color in the design section.

How much is a gender reveal cake?

The same as any other cake: $65 and up for a 6-inch, 2-layer cake. See all prices.
