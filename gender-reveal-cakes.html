Gender Reveal FAQ

Can the reveal color be hidden inside the cake?

Mention it in your quote request and I'll let you know what's possible for your design.

Can someone else place the order so the gender stays a secret?

Of course. Whoever knows the answer can fill out the quote form and list the reveal color in the design section.

How much is a gender reveal cake?

Gender reveal cakes use the same pricing as all my cakes, starting at $65 for a 6-inch, 2-layer cake. See the full price list.
