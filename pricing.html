How ordering works

  1. Pick a date. I take orders up to 1½ months before your pickup date and at least 3 days out. Dates further than 1½ months away can't be confirmed yet.
  2. Request a quote. Send your date, guest count, colors and message through the quote form. "Not sure yet" is fine for anything you haven't decided.
  3. Book it. I reply within 24 hours with your price. A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves your date, and the rest is due 24 hours before pickup.
  4. Pick up your cake. Cakes are made for pickup at your scheduled time. Delivery is very limited, so ask before you book.

Questions

How far ahead can I order?

I take orders up to 1½ months before your pickup date, and at least 3 days out. Dates further than 1½ months away can't be confirmed yet, so please send your request once you're inside that window.

Does a quote hold my date?

No. Your date is reserved once you place the order and pay the 50% non-refundable deposit.

When is the rest of the payment due?

24 hours before pickup.

How many people will my cake serve?

Put your guest count in the "How many people" field on the quote form and I'll suggest a size and number of layers.

Do you deliver?

Cakes are made for pickup, with pickup times from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Delivery is very limited, isn't guaranteed and has a mileage fee.

What areas do you serve?

I'm a home bakery in Tampa, and customers pick up from all over Tampa Bay, including St. Petersburg and Clearwater.

Can you work around allergies?

List any allergies or dietary restrictions on the quote form and I'll tell you what I can do for your cake.

How should I transport and store my cake?

Buttercream softens in heat. Carry the cake flat in an air-conditioned car, keep it out of the sun, and keep it cool until you serve it. Once a cake is picked up or delivered, I'm not responsible for damage in transport.

Can I ask for a decoration that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose "Custom Add On" on the quote form and message me on Instagram with the idea.
