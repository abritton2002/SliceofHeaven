Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

Order requests need a date at least 3 days out. Popular dates fill up, so reach out as early as you can, especially for weekends and weddings.

Does a quote reserve my date?

No. A quote gives you pricing. Your date is reserved once you place an order and pay the 50% non-refundable deposit.

When is the final payment due?

The remaining balance is due 24 hours before pickup.

How many people will my cake serve?

Put your guest count in the "How many people" field on the quote form and I'll recommend the right size and number of layers.

Do you deliver?

Cakes are made for pickup, with pickup times from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Delivery is very limited and not guaranteed. When it's available, there's an extra fee based on mileage.

What areas do you serve?

I'm a home-based bakery in Tampa and serve customers across Tampa Bay, including St. Petersburg and Clearwater.

Can you accommodate allergies?

List any allergies or dietary restrictions on the quote form and I'll tell you what I can accommodate for your cake.

How should I store and transport my cake?

Buttercream is heat-sensitive, especially in Florida. Keep your cake cool and out of direct sun, carry it flat in an air-conditioned car, and store it properly until serving. Once a cake is picked up or delivered, I'm no longer responsible for damage during transport.

How do I ask for a design that's not listed?

Choose "Custom Add On" on the quote form and message me on Instagram with your idea.
