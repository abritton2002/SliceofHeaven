Birthday cake questions

How far ahead should I order?

I take orders up to 1½ months before your pickup date, and at least 3 days out. Weekends book up first, so earlier in that window is better.

Can you match my party theme?

Yes. Put your theme and colors on the quote form. For decorations that aren't on the form, message me on Instagram.

Do you deliver?

Cakes are made for pickup. Delivery is very limited, isn't guaranteed and has a mileage fee, so ask before you order.
