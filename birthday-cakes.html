A Birthday Cake Made Just for Them

How to Order

  1. Get inspired. Browse the gallery and save the designs you love.
  2. Request a quote. Fill out the quote form with your date, guest count, colors and message. It's fine if some details are still "not sure yet."
  3. Confirm your date. I'll reply within 24 hours with pricing. A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves your date, and the balance is due 24 hours before pickup.
  4. Pick up your cake. Your cake will be ready at your scheduled pickup time. Delivery is very limited, so please ask before you book.

Birthday Cake FAQ

How far in advance should I order a birthday cake?

Order requests need a date at least 3 days out, but the sooner you reach out, the better your chances of getting your date. Weekends go first.

Can you match my party theme?

Yes. Share your colors, theme and any inspiration on the quote form. For custom decorations not listed on the form, message me on Instagram.

What flavors can I choose?

Vanilla, chocolate, red velvet, carrot, confetti and lemon are included. Oreo vanilla cake and specialty fillings are available for an extra charge. See the flavors & pricing page.

Do you deliver birthday cakes?

Cakes are made for pickup. Delivery is very limited and not guaranteed. When it's available, it has an extra fee based on mileage, so please ask before you order.
